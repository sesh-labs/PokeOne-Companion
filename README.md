# PokeOne Companion

A free, offline companion app to keep open while you play **PokéOne**. It shows the wild Pokémon, items, people and gym where you are, tracks your badges, team and goals, and has a searchable Pokédex. It uses game information from the official PokéOne website.

> **Private beta.** Only invited testers can see this page. This is an early test version. Expect rough edges, and please report what you find.
> PokeOne Companion is an unofficial fan-made tool, not affiliated with PokéOne, Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.

## Install and run

1. Right-click the downloaded zip and choose **Extract All…**. Pick a folder you'll remember, for example `Documents\PokeOne Companion`.
2. Open the extracted folder `PokeOne-Companion-0.1.5-beta-win-x64` and double-click **`PokeOne Companion.exe`**.
3. The first time, Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. This beta isn't digitally signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
4. Choose **I'm new to PokéOne** or **I'm already playing**, and follow the short setup. New players start in Pallet Town (Kanto) or New Bark Town (Johto), where the app shows the three starters.

Your progress saves automatically on your own computer (in `%APPDATA%\PokeOne Companion`). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**Updating from an earlier version:** first save a backup file (**Settings → Backups → Save a backup file…**). Then extract the new zip to a new folder and run it from there. Your profiles carry over; you can delete the old folder afterwards.

**Removing it:** delete the app's folder, and, if you want your progress gone too, the folder `%APPDATA%\PokeOne Companion` (type that into File Explorer's address bar). The guide's "Removing everything" section has the details.

The full **[Beta Tester Guide](GUIDE.md)** is also inside the zip and attached to the release.

## Play-testing: your first hour

1. Set up a profile the way you really play: as a new player, or with your real progress (region, badges, trainer level).
2. Play PokéOne with the app open beside the game. Press **Change location** whenever you move, and check the wild Pokémon, items and people against what you actually see.
3. On **Team**, add your real party; give two of them levels, natures and moves, and try the **EV plan** on one.
4. On **Progress**, mark your badges. Does the next-gym suggestion make sense?
5. In the **Pokédex**, look up three Pokémon you want (try Eevee and Pikachu too): where to find them, the evolution pictures, the moves.
6. In **Settings → Backups**, save a backup file, then restore it.
7. Close the app, open it again, and check that everything is still there.
8. Tell us what you looked for and didn't find, what confused you, and a score out of 10 for "would I keep this open while playing?".

## Report a problem or suggest an idea

Use **[Issues](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/issues)** → **New issue**, and pick a form: bug report, wrong game information, or an idea. You can also use **Settings → Feedback & bug reports → Report a problem on GitHub** inside the app. Please include **Settings → Feedback & bug reports → Copy diagnostic information** (if the app won't open, just say so). For wrong game information, say where it was and what the game showed. Other invited testers can see reports, so don't include anything private, and crop screenshots to the app window.
