# PokeOne Companion

A free app to keep open beside PokéOne. It shows what lives where you are, keeps track of your team, badges, dailies and bosses, and tells you if your party is ready for the next gym. It works offline, and its game information comes from the official PokéOne website.

This is a private beta, so only invited testers can see this page. Expect rough edges, and please tell us what you find. It's an unofficial fan project, not connected to PokéOne, Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.

## Download

Sign in to GitHub with the account you were invited with, and accept the invitation first ([your invitations](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/invitations)). The downloads only work while you're signed in.

**[Download 0.2.1-beta](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/releases/tag/v0.2.1-beta)**, then under Assets pick `PokeOne-Companion-0.2.1-beta-win-x64.zip` (about 160 MB).

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 550 MB of free space.

SHA-256 checksum, if you want to check the file: `CHECKSUM_AT_PUBLISH`

Older versions are on the [releases page](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/releases).

## Install

1. Right-click the zip, choose Extract All…, and pick a folder you'll remember.
2. Open the new folder and double-click `PokeOne Companion.exe`.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The beta isn't signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it.
4. Pick "I'm new to PokéOne" or "I'm already playing" and follow the short setup.

Your progress stays on your own computer, in `%APPDATA%\PokeOne Companion`. Nothing is uploaded.

**Updating?** Save a backup first (Settings → Backups → Save a backup file…), then extract the new zip to a new folder and open it there. Your profiles come along. The [guide](GUIDE.md) explains going back to an older version and removing the app.

## Testing it

Play with the app open beside the game, or in the mini view on top of it (Ctrl+M). Set it up the way you really play, add your real team and Bag, and press Change location when you move. Then tell us:

- what you looked for and couldn't find
- what confused you or felt slow
- anything that doesn't match the game
- out of 10, would you keep it open while you play?

## Report something

Use [Issues](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/issues) → New issue and pick a form, or in the app go to Settings → Feedback & bug reports. For a bug, paste the diagnostic information from that same place. Other testers can read your reports, so leave out anything private and crop screenshots to the app window.

Some things the official site doesn't say, so the app marks them "check in game". If you know one of the [open questions](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), use the "I know the answer" form.
