# PokeOne Companion

A free app to keep open beside PokéOne. It shows what lives where you are, keeps track of your team, badges, dailies and bosses, and tells you if your party is ready for the next gym. It works offline, and its game information comes from the official PokéOne website.

This is a private beta for invited testers, so expect rough edges. It's an unofficial fan project, not connected to PokéOne, Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.

## 1. Download

Sign in to GitHub with the account you were invited with (accept [your invitation](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/invitations) first). Open **[0.2.3-beta](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/releases/tag/v0.2.3-beta)** and under Assets pick `PokeOne-Companion-0.2.3-beta-win-x64.zip` (about 160 MB). You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 550 MB of free space.

## 2. Install

Right-click the zip, choose Extract All…, then double-click `PokeOne Companion.exe` in the new folder. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway (the beta isn't signed yet). Updating? Extract it to a new folder and run it from there; your profiles come along.

## 3. Tell us

Play with the app open beside the game, or in the mini view on top of it (Ctrl+M). Then open [Issues](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/issues) → New issue, or in the app Settings → Feedback & bug reports, and tell us what you couldn't find, what confused you or felt slow, anything that doesn't match the game, and out of 10, whether you'd keep it open while you play.

## What's new in 0.2.3

HM steps now count only when a Pokémon in your party knows the move, as in the game, and an HM mule waiting outside your party gets a "put it in your party" hint. Field tools like the Bat and the Hammer count everywhere. The daily reset countdown sits at the bottom of the menu on every page, and you can add an item to your Bag from the search box with Shift+Enter. Unova's trainer level caps your Pokémon too. The [release notes](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/releases/tag/v0.2.3-beta) have the full list.
