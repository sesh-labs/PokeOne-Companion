# Open questions: help us check the game

*Made 2026-09-29 from the app's data (2026-09-22). 84 open questions. This list shrinks as answers come in.*

The companion takes its facts from the official PokéOne site, and sometimes the site contradicts itself or says nothing. You can settle it by looking in the game.

**How to answer:** open the form [**"I know the answer"**](https://github.com/sesh-labs/PokeOne-Companion/issues/new?template=4-answer-a-question.yml) (on the app's GitHub page: Issues › New issue), paste the question's id (the bold code, like **DAILY-ghost**) and say what you saw. A screenshot helps, cropped to the game window. Never include your name, other players' names or your computer's file paths.

Each line reads: **id** · where to go · what to look at · *what the app says now*. Answer as many or as few as you like; one answer is already a big help.
